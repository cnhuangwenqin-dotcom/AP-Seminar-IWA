# Output Template and Comment Patterns

Use this template for detailed IWA review. Adapt length to the user's needs, but keep the row-by-row structure.

## Required opening stance

- Say the score is predicted and not official.
- State whether the evaluation is based on the full draft, partial draft, or missing references.
- If citations, bibliography, stimulus packet year, or research question are missing, say how that lowers confidence.

## CB-style phrasing patterns

Use precise language like:

- "This currently looks more like evidence-driven reporting than a student-driven argument."
- "The source is relevant, but the commentary stops before explaining how it changes the answer to the research question."
- "This paragraph names two perspectives, but it does not yet place them in dialogue."
- "The draft has a plausible conclusion, but it is not yet fully aligned with the research question because..."
- "The stimulus material is present, but it functions as a hook rather than as part of the argument."
- "The bibliography suggests credible research, but the paper does not consistently establish why each source should carry weight."
- "The student voice is strongest when the paragraph explains the implication of the evidence rather than moving immediately to the next citation."

## What not to do

- Do not say only "add more commentary." Explain where and what kind: relevance, implication, limitation, connection to claim, relationship to another source, or link back to research question.
- Do not rewrite a full section for the student.
- Do not claim College Board would definitely assign the same score.
- Do not reward source count alone.
- Do not treat lenses such as "economic" or "social" as perspectives unless the lens is attached to a source-based argument.
